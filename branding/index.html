cara mia — Italian for my beloved. The Q holds a C the way a heart holds love; the gradient is a sunrise — gold radiance giving way to crimson devotion.

Concept

A monogram that whispers cara mia

  • Q
    The outer ring is the Q A complete circle with a diagonal tail. Renders Qaramia at a glance, anchored by the brand letter.
  • C
    The inner arc is the C Nested inside the Q, opening to the right — the visual hint to cara mia. Two letters, one mark.
  • ☀
    Sunrise gradient = radiance Gold to peach to rose on the outer ring. The hour just after dawn, when light is warmest.
  • ♡
    Crimson interior = love The C burns hotter — rose to deep crimson — placing the heart at the centre of the composition.
  • ✦
    Halo for warmth A soft radial glow surrounds the mark. Sun-warmed skin, candlelight, an opened love letter.
Background Variants

The mark holds up across every variant in the palette — cream-warm for primary use, sunset gradient for hero moments, deep wine for night-mode and elevated contexts.
