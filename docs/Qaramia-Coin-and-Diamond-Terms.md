## 1. Definitions

**"Platform"** means the Qaramia website, mobile applications, and any Qaramia-operated services.

**"Coins"** means the virtual, non-refundable, non-transferable platform credit that Users may purchase to access certain in-Platform features, including the sending of Gifts. Coins are a limited-purpose digital token and **are not legal tender, electronic money, cryptocurrency, or any form of monetary instrument**. Coins have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money, goods, or services outside of the Platform.

**"Diamonds"** means the creator-side reward unit accumulated by Creators when Viewers send Gifts to their live streams. Diamonds are an internal accounting unit reflecting a Creator's eligibility to receive a Creator Payout. Diamonds are **not money or currency** and have no value outside the Platform's redemption mechanism.

**"Gift"** means any digital good, animation, or stream effect that Users may send to a Creator during a live stream by spending Coins.

**"Creator"** means a User who broadcasts live content on the Platform and is eligible to receive Diamonds.

**"Viewer"** means a User who watches live content and may purchase Coins and send Gifts.

**"Creator Payout"** means the cash payment Qaramia makes to a Creator in exchange for redemption of accumulated Diamonds, subject to the terms below.

## 2. Nature of Coins

2.1 **Coins are not money.** By purchasing Coins, you acknowledge and agree that Coins are a non-refundable, non-transferable digital token that grants you a limited license to access certain in-Platform features. Coins are not, and shall not be construed as, currency, e-money, a stored-value instrument, a security, a deposit, or any other regulated financial instrument.

2.2 **Coins are non-refundable** once credited to your Wallet, except where mandated by applicable consumer-protection law (see Section 8).

2.3 **Coins are non-transferable.** You may not sell, gift, trade, or transfer Coins to any other User, person, or party. Any attempt to do so will result in immediate forfeiture of the Coins and may result in suspension or termination of your Account.

2.4 **Coins have no cash value.** Qaramia does not, and will not, redeem Coins for cash, goods, or services outside of the Platform. Coins are consumed when used to send a Gift and cannot be recovered.

2.5 **Coins do not constitute a debt of Qaramia.** Qaramia's only obligation in respect of Coins is to make them available for use within the Platform in accordance with these Terms.

2.6 **Coin expiry.** Coins expire and are automatically forfeited if your Account is inactive (no log-in) for a continuous period of twenty-four (24) months. Expiry creates no obligation on Qaramia to refund or credit you in any form.

## 3. Nature of Diamonds

3.1 **Diamonds are not money.** Diamonds represent a Creator's eligibility to receive a Creator Payout from Qaramia, calculated at a conversion rate determined by Qaramia in its sole discretion. Diamonds are not currency, e-money, or any regulated financial instrument.

3.2 **Diamond conversion rate.** The Diamond-to-cash conversion rate is set by Qaramia and may be amended from time to time on thirty (30) days' notice. As of the date of these Terms, the standard rate is one Diamond = one United States cent (USD $0.01) for Creators in the Standard tier, with higher rates for Partner and Top-100 Creator tiers as published in the Creator Programme.

3.3 **Diamonds are non-transferable** between Creators or other persons.

3.4 **Diamond redemption requires a minimum balance** of 5,000 Diamonds (the equivalent of USD $50 at the Standard conversion rate). Balances below the minimum will remain in the Creator's account until the threshold is reached or the account becomes inactive.

3.5 **Inactive Creator accounts.** If a Creator account is inactive (no log-in or content broadcast) for a continuous period of twelve (12) months, Diamonds may be forfeited at Qaramia's discretion following thirty (30) days' written notice to the Creator's registered email address.

You are responsible for ensuring that the payment method you use is valid and that any fees charged by your card issuer or bank are paid.

4.3 **Coin credit.** Coins are credited to your Wallet immediately upon successful payment confirmation from the payment processor. If a payment is later reversed, charged back, or refunded for any reason, Qaramia reserves the right to deduct the equivalent Coins (and any Diamonds awarded from Gifts funded by those Coins) from the relevant accounts.

4.4 **Limits.** Qaramia may impose, at its discretion, daily or monthly Coin-purchase limits on individual Accounts to protect Users from excessive spending. Users may also voluntarily set their own spending limits via the Spend Controls in their Account Settings.

4.5 **Promotional bonuses.** Qaramia may from time to time offer bonus Coins as part of promotional coin packs. Bonus Coins are subject to all the same terms as purchased Coins, including non-refundability.

## 5. Gift Sending

5.1 **Use of Coins.** Coins may be used solely to send Gifts to Creators on the Platform. Once a Gift is sent, the Coins consumed are non-recoverable.

5.2 **Acknowledgement of gift.** Sending a Gift is a voluntary expression of support for a Creator. Qaramia makes no representation or warranty that any Creator will respond, acknowledge, or reciprocate any Gift.

5.3 **No purchase of goods or services.** Sending a Gift does not constitute the purchase of any physical or digital product from a Creator or from Qaramia. Gifts are virtual animations and on-stream effects only.

5.4 **Sponsored Gifts.** Some Gifts may be designated as sponsored Gifts and may carry the branding of a third-party advertiser. Sponsored Gifts may be available at a reduced Coin cost or free of charge to Viewers, in which case Qaramia may receive payment from the relevant advertiser. The Creator's Diamond yield from a sponsored Gift may differ from the standard yield and will be disclosed in the Creator Programme.

5.5 **Prohibited use.** You agree not to use Coins or Gifts to: (a) harass, threaten, defame, or impersonate any person; (b) circumvent any Platform feature designed to limit spending; (c) launder money or proceeds of crime; (d) violate any applicable law or regulation.

## 6. Creator Programme

6.1 **Eligibility.** To redeem Diamonds for cash, a Creator must be at least eighteen (18) years of age, have a verified Account, have agreed to the Creator Programme Terms, and have completed Know-Your-Customer (KYC) verification through Qaramia's payment processor.

6.2 **Tax responsibility.** Creator Payouts may constitute taxable income in the Creator's jurisdiction. Creators are solely responsible for the declaration and payment of all applicable taxes. Qaramia will issue tax forms (US: Form 1099-K; EU: DAC7 report; equivalent forms in other jurisdictions) where required by law.

6.3 **Payout method.** Creator Payouts are made via Stripe Connect bank transfer or such other method as Qaramia may from time to time make available. Payouts are typically processed monthly, NET 30 from the end of the earning period.

6.4 **Withholding and chargebacks.** Qaramia reserves the right to withhold Creator Payouts pending investigation of suspected fraud, violation of the Creator Programme Terms, or chargeback activity on Coin purchases that funded the underlying Gifts.

9.3 **Self-imposed limits.** You may set daily or monthly Coin-purchase limits in your Account Settings. Such limits, once set, may be reduced at any time and increased only after a 24-hour cooling-off period.

9.4 **Cool-down.** After any single Coin pack purchase of USD $50 or more, no further Coin purchase may be made for a period of five (5) minutes from the time of the original purchase.

## 10. Prohibition on Gambling Mechanics

10.1 **No random rewards.** All Gifts and Coin packs sold on the Platform are deterministic. Qaramia does not offer "loot boxes," random-content Gift packs, or any feature whose outcome is determined by chance.

10.2 **No conversion of Coins to cash.** Coins, once purchased, may only be consumed within the Platform. No mechanism exists or will be created to enable Viewers to convert Coins back to fiat currency.

10.3 **Single-direction value flow.** Value flows from Viewer to Creator via Gift, and from Qaramia to Creator via Creator Payout. There is no User-to-User cash flow. This structure is intentional and designed to ensure that Coins do not function as a means of payment or store of value.
