Why Now

Three forces converging in 2025

🧠

AI vision is finally accurate enough

Claude Vision, GPT-4o, and barcode SDKs now identify products in real-world, imperfect lighting with >90% accuracy. This wasn't true two years ago.

🌏

The Western market is wide open

Live commerce is $700B in Asia but only $35B in the US — not because demand doesn't exist, but because no platform has nailed the product. TikTok Shop proved Americans will buy on live video.

A potential US ban leaves 170M TikTok users and tens of thousands of live commerce creators actively looking for an alternative. The window to capture them is now.
