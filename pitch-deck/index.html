Product

A full creator studio — built for commerce from day one

🔍

Visual Product Scanner

Barcode (instant, free) + Claude Vision fallback. Identifies branded goods, food, electronics, fashion from any frame the camera sees.

🎙

Live Captions + Spoken Product Detection

Real-time speech-to-text via Web Speech API. Captions improve accessibility and session length; the same transcript triggers ads when products are mentioned, not just shown.

🎯

Multimodal Ad Matching

Visual + audio signals feed one ad matcher. Advertisers bid on keywords or categories; CPMs 3–5× platform average; spoken endorsements command a 1.5–2× premium.

✨

Live Commerce Engine

Snap AR lenses, video filters, virtual backgrounds, co-host room mode, danmaku chat, coin/diamond gifting with Stripe payout, sponsored gifts tied to detected products.

Why Now

Four forces converging in 2026

🧠

Multimodal AI is the new battleground

Claude Vision, GPT-4o, Gemini Live — all shipped vision + audio in 2024/25. Web Speech API now ships natively in every major browser. The infrastructure to identify products from both pixels and speech is production-ready for the first time.

🌏

The Western market is wide open

Live commerce is $700B in Asia but only $35B in the US — not because demand doesn't exist, but because no platform has nailed the product. TikTok Shop proved Americans will buy on live video.

♿

Accessibility is becoming a regulatory floor

EU Accessibility Act (in force June 2025), expanded ADA digital rules, UK Equality Act enforcement — every consumer platform now needs auto-captions. Qaramia ships this as a feature, not a compliance burden.

📵

TikTok's regulatory uncertainty creates a vacuum

The ongoing US scrutiny and the broader trust gap leave 170M TikTok users and tens of thousands of live commerce creators actively looking for an alternative.
